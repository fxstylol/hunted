Hunted

Run or be eaten.

Hunted turns every run into a chase. Outrun a cheetah and you've done sprint intervals. Outlast a wolf pack and that was a tempo run. Hunt a gazelle and it's a progression run.

Fifty animals on each side

Be hunted by predators or become one. Every animal runs the way it does in the wild, scaled to your own easy pace, so the chase is always hard enough to matter.

On your iPhone and Apple Watch

Live alerts as the animal closes in, trophies, streaks, weekly leaderboards and runs saved to Apple Health.
