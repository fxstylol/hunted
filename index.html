How a run goes

Start. Pocket it. Run.

No staring at a screen. Hunted coaches the whole chase so your eyes stay on the road.

  1. 1

    Warm up

    A few easy minutes first. Nothing is out there yet.

  2. 2

    It appears

    Three long buzzes. The round is on, with a head start from a predator or a lead your prey has on you.

  3. 3

    Hold or close the gap

    The animal speeds up and tires the way the real one does. Run the round's pace to win it.

  4. 4

    Recover, repeat

    An easy jog between rounds, then the next one hits. Or set a distance or time goal for one long chase.
